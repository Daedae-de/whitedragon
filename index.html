ABOUT WHITE DRAGON

Our origins, competitive ethos, and community.
"United under the banner of the White Dragon, we strive for competitive excellence, relentless improvement, and unyielding brotherhood across the Koprulu sector."

Founded in 2021, White Dragon [WD] is an international StarCraft: Remastered competitive clan home to passionate Terran, Protoss, and Zerg commanders from around the globe.

Our primary tournament roster—WD-A—actively battles in the Brood War Clan League (BWCL), proudly contending in Division D. Beyond competitive clan wars, our home is open to players who respect the classic craft of Brood War and love the game.

COMPETITIVE SPIRIT

Rigorous practice, replay study, and team coordination to bring out our best in every BWCL match.

GLOBAL BROTHERHOOD

A welcoming clan spanning Germany, Canada, the USA, the Philippines, Mexico, and beyond.

BROOD WAR PASSION

Preserving the legacy of the greatest real-time strategy game ever created through community and sportsmanship.
