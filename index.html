ABOUT WHITE DRAGON

Our origins, community spirit, and clan family.
"More than just a clan tag—a global family united by our love for Brood War, friendly banter, and good games."

Founded in 2021, White Dragon [WD] began with a simple idea: bringing together friendly commanders from across the globe who love StarCraft: Remastered without the pressure of hardcore elitism.

We believe the best clan experience isn't just about winning games—it’s about who you share them with. While our competitive squad WD-A proudly represents our banner in the Brood War Clan League (BWCL), our heart is a casual, tight-knit family. We play weekly in-house matches, team games, and practice sessions where everyone helps each other improve at their own pace.

Whether you’re a returning veteran from the 90s, a casual ladder player, or someone who simply enjoys watching replays and hanging out on Discord, White Dragon is a home where every member has a seat at the table.

FAMILY FIRST

A supportive, drama-free community where every player is treated with respect and friendship, win or lose.

CASUAL & COMPETITIVE BALANCE

Enjoy relaxed in-house games and casual 2v2s, or test your skills alongside teammates in official clan wars.

BROOD WAR PASSION

Keeping the legacy of the classic RTS alive through good sportsmanship, laughter, and shared improvement.

Privacy | White Dragon Clan

Important information about privacy using this site!

💾 1. CLIENT-SIDE LOCAL STORAGE

Any match replays (.rep) or map packs (.scx / .zip) you upload through our terminal are kept directly inside your own web browser via local storage (localStorage). They are not sold, transmitted to third-party ad networks, or mined for personal data.

🛡️ 2. NO COMMERCIAL TRACKING & NO COOKIE PROFILING

White Dragon [WD] operates as an independent, non-profit StarCraft eSports gaming community. We do not use intrusive commercial advertising trackers or marketing analytics to track your personal identity.

🌐 3. EXTERNAL LINKS (DISCORD & BLIZZARD)

Our website provides direct links to Discord and Battle.net for recruitment and match coordination. Interaction with those external platforms is subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

💬 4. COMMUNITY PRIVACY CONTACT

If you have any questions or would like your player alias or member profile removed from our public roster, message clan administrators directly on Discord (Death~Orb or RobFATKraft).
